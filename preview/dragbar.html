Auto-hiding top drag strip

Move your pointer to the very top edge of the white page below — the strip slides down. Move away and it hides again after 3 seconds. Click the pin icon to keep it open permanently. In the real app the strip is an overlay only 6px tall while hidden, so the page keeps receiving essentially every pointer event.

The quick brown fox

Preview article · 3 min read

This stands in for a real webpage. In MiniBrowser the page window is completely frameless — no title bar, no borders, no native menu — so the page is rendered edge to edge.

That left one gap: with no title bar there was nothing to grab to move the window. The auto-hiding strip above fills it without permanently stealing any space from the page.

It carries the page title plus minimize, maximize and close buttons, and it can be switched off entirely from the Window menu on the floating control bar.
